# The Pathfinder — original world artwork

`pathfinder-world.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool for this portfolio. It is conceptual world artwork, not a screenshot or a claim of the game's rendering fidelity.

Final prompt: Cinematic dark fantasy valley at blue hour, monumental amber stone portal on right, ancient bridge and tiny traveler, layered misty mountains, midnight teal palette, detailed painterly realism, quiet dark left third, no text or interface.

The full creation brief requested a weathered stone portal, a bridge across a gorge, a tiny traveler for scale, windblown grass, restrained volumetric lighting, midnight teal and gold, and open space on the left for an ivory headline. No logos, text, or watermarks.

Asset dimensions: 1672 × 941. The original asset is preserved without image edits.
